

## 1. What is CRM

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

It is a system used by companies to manage customer information, sales, communication, and services in one place.

CRM helps businesses improve customer relationships and increase sales efficiency.



## 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform.

Companies use Salesforce because it helps them:

- Store customer information
- Track sales opportunities
- Improve customer support
- Automate business processes
- Generate reports and analytics

Salesforce is widely used because it is secure, scalable, and accessible from anywhere.



## 3. Salesforce Objects

### Account

An Account represents a company or organization.

Examples:
- Amazon
- Infosys
- TCS

Accounts store business information such as company name, industry, and location.



### Contact

A Contact represents a person associated with an Account.

Examples:
- Employee
- Customer
- Manager

Contacts contain information like phone number, email, and communication details.


### Opportunity

An Opportunity represents a potential sales deal.

It helps companies track:
- Deal amount
- Deal stage
- Expected closing date

Sales teams use Opportunities to manage sales progress.



## 4. Real-World Mapping

- Real World ->Salesforce Object 
-Company -> Account 
-Person working in company -> Contact 
- Business deal or sale -> Opportunity 

Example:
- Amazon → Account
- HR Manager at Amazon → Contact
- Software Project Deal → Opportunity
